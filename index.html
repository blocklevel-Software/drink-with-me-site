Our point of view

Your social life should not happen inside a feed.

We do not want you to scroll endlessly, perform for an audience, or swipe through people forever. Drink With Me is designed to get out of the way once it has helped something real happen.

Social Pulse

The right nudge at the right moment.

Social Pulse brings together privacy-safe signals such as who is open, what people feel like doing, nearby happenings, buddies, and mutual interest. It looks for one useful opportunity, not another infinite list.

  1. 01

    Share a lightweight intent

    Say that you are open, up for a casual drink, coffee, live music, or simply open to ideas.

  2. 02

    Notice what aligns

    Social Pulse finds a timely, explainable opportunity without exposing private one-sided interest.

  3. 03

    Make it happen

    Turn the moment into a plan, put the phone away, and meet in real life.
