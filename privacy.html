Privacy

Privacy matters in real life, too.

Drink With Me uses profile, preference, and rough-area information to provide discovery, plans, messaging, and relevant social opportunities.

Location

Exact live location is not shown to other users. The app uses broad areas or privacy-safe distances where location context is useful.

Private intent

One-sided interest and private social signals are not revealed. Social Pulse presents an opportunity only when the product's privacy rules permit it.

Your account

You can download a copy of your account data or delete your account from Account & data in the app. Essential security, fraud-prevention, and service records may be retained where required by law.

Authentication

Sign-in is handled by Microsoft Entra External ID. Drink With Me does not ask for your Microsoft password on this website.
