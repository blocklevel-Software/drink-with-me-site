FAQ

Good questions.

Is this a dating app?

No. It’s about new acquaintances and friendships. If it turns into more – lovely. You can add “Dates ok” to your profile, but you don’t have to.

Do I have to drink alcohol?

No. Coffee, mate, lemonade or a mocktail: “drink” simply means a drink.

Who sees my location?

Strangers only see the neighbourhood, friends see the bar. You share your location only if you want – once, in a chat. After 60 minutes it’s gone.

Who is Drink With Me for?

For everyone 18+. Especially for people between 25 and 45 who are new in town or want to widen their circle.

Where is the app available?

In public beta for iPhone and Android, in Germany and Austria. We’re launching in Vienna, Düsseldorf and Willich.
